Patents · prior art for disputes

A Japanese patent cited as prior art

Part of a patent publication that one side cites as prior art in a patent dispute. The English goes in as evidence, and the other side checks every word. So it comes with notes for counsel on terms the other side could dispute, and a signed declaration.

Notes for counsel

1. 異常度 (claim 1, paragraphs [0015]–[0021]): rendered “degree of anomaly” throughout. “Anomaly score” is common in the field, but a score suggests a number on a fixed scale. The reference doesn’t say the value has one.

2. 特徴量 (claim 1, paragraph [0015]): rendered “a feature value”, singular. The Japanese doesn’t mark number. Paragraph [0016] calculates one value from each measurement.

Sent to you with the translation. You decide whether they’re filed.

Declaration of translator

I, [Translator], declare that I am fluent in Japanese and English and competent to translate from Japanese into English, and that the attached English translation of JP 2019-000001 A is complete and accurate to the best of my knowledge and belief. I declare under penalty of perjury under the laws of the United States of America that the foregoing is true and correct.

Executed on [date] · Signature

  • Claim structure kept: one element per line, in standard claim English
  • Paragraph numbers and reference numerals kept, so every point can be checked against the original
  • Notes for counsel on any term the other side could dispute
  • A signed declaration of accuracy, as the PTAB requires (37 CFR 42.63(b))
