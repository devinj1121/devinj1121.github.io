Questions

Do the translations need to be notarized?

No. USCIS asks for a translator’s certificate, and every order includes one.

Can you translate only part of a long paper or patent?

No. USCIS rejects summaries. If an English version already exists, such as a US family patent, we’ll point it out.

Can the applicant order directly?

Yes. The translation can go straight to their lawyer.

Do you take rush orders?

Delivery in 3 business days or fewer adds 50%. There are no 24-hour orders.

Do the tools use AI?

Parts of them do. They flag problems and your reviewers decide. Each flag points to its source page, so a reviewer can check it, as ABA Formal Opinion 512 expects.

Do you give legal advice?

No. Decisions about the case stay with the attorney.
