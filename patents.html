Court and PTAB

Prior art translation

A Japanese or Chinese reference only helps your case once it’s in English. The other side will check every word of that English. At the PTAB, a non-English exhibit also needs a translation and an affidavit of accuracy (37 CFR 42.63(b)).

Sen Linguistics translates the full patent publication or paper, and signs a declaration of accuracy. Terms that could be read two ways are flagged in notes for counsel, before the other side finds them.

Reexamination requests

A reexam request needs English for only the “necessary and pertinent parts” of a non-English reference (37 CFR 1.510(b)(3)). No declaration is required.

Counsel marks the parts that matter. Sen Linguistics translates those parts, with paragraph numbers kept so the examiner can find them in the original. You pay only for the words translated.

Questions

Is a machine translation enough?

Not as filed evidence at the PTAB. Rule 42.63(b) asks for a translation and an affidavit of accuracy. In IPR2020-00670, institution was denied for a petition that relied on machine translations without an affidavit. A machine translation can help you choose which references to cite. Send the ones you cite for translation.

Can you testify about the translation?

Yes. The translator who signed the declaration is available for deposition. The hourly rate is quoted per case.

What’s different for a reexam request?

A reexam request needs English for only the necessary and pertinent parts of a reference, and no declaration. A PTAB exhibit needs a translation and an affidavit of accuracy. The rate is the same. A reexam costs less because fewer words are translated.

Do you translate Chinese patents?

Yes, in the same fields, at the same price and with the same declaration.

What if a reference has an English family member?

If a US or other English-language family member exists, we’ll point it out. Counsel decides whether it can stand in for a translation.

Can you meet a filing deadline?

Tell us the filing date when you ask for a quote. Delivery in 3 business days or fewer adds 50%. There are no 24-hour orders.

Can you help sort a long list of references?

Yes. For a long list of Japanese and Chinese references from a search, we can sort which ones bear on the claims and which are worth translating in full. Describe the list in the quote form.

Do you give legal advice?

No. Sen Linguistics provides independent translation. The notes for counsel cover language only. Claim construction and case decisions stay with counsel.
