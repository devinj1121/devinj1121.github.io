Paragraph [0012]

Google Translate

The1 translation apparatus comprises: an acquisition unit that obtains a translation3; a terminology storage unit that stores terms in association with their corresponding translations3; a search unit that searches the terminology storage unit for terms2 contained in the original text1; and a translation unit that generates a translation3 such that the translations of the terms found3 by the search unit are included in the translated text.

Paragraph [0012]
…with the translations of the terms retrieved3 by the search unit 13 serving as a constraint.

Sen Linguistics

A1 translation device comprising: an acquisition unit that acquires a source text3; a term storage unit that stores terms in association with equivalents3 of the terms; a search unit that searches the term storage unit for a term2 included in the source text1; and a translation unit that translates the source text such that the equivalent of the term found3 by the search unit is included in a translated text.

Paragraph [0012]
…using the equivalent of the term found3 by the search unit 13 as a constraint.

  1. 1“A” and “the”. In a claim, “a” introduces something and “the” points back to it. Google starts with “The”, which points back to nothing.
  2. 2Plurals. Japanese doesn’t mark them. Google’s “terms” changes what the claim covers.
  3. 3Consistency. Google uses “translation” for three different things, and “found” becomes “retrieved” in paragraph [0012].

3

Notes for counsel

Terms the other side could dispute, flagged first

The other side will check every word of the English. Notes cover language only. Claim construction stays with counsel.

Notes for counsel · JP 2019-000001 A

1. 訳語 (claim 1, paragraph [0012]): rendered “equivalent”, to keep it apart from 訳文 (“translated text”). Both could be read as “translation”.

2. 復号 (paragraph [0018]): rendered “decoding”. In cryptography the word means “decryption”. Here it refers to a translation model generating output, for which the standard English term is “decoding”.
