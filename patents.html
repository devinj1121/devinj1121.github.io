2

Translation

Each sentence reads fine in machine English. As a claim, it breaks.

Here is claim 1 and paragraph [0015] of the first reference, in Google Translate’s English and in ours. Claims keep one element per line. Paragraph numbers and reference numerals stay. A term gets the same English in every reference.

Japanese

センサから計測値を取得する取得部と、前記計測値から特徴量を算出する算出部と、学習済みモデルを用いて前記特徴量から異常度を推定する推定部と、前記異常度が閾値を超えた場合に警報を出力する出力部と、を備える異常検知装置。

Paragraph [0015]
推定部14は、算出部13が算出した特徴量を学習済みモデルに入力し、当該特徴量の異常度を推定する。異常度が高いほど、計測値が正常時の分布から外れていることを示す。

Google Translate

An anomaly detection device comprising: an acquisition unit that obtains measurement values1 from a sensor; a calculation unit that computes feature values1 from said measurement values; an estimation unit that estimates an anomaly score2 from said feature values using a trained model; and an output unit that issues an alarm when said anomaly score exceeds a threshold value.

Paragraph [0015]
The estimation unit 14 inputs the feature values calculated by the calculation unit 13 into a trained model and estimates the degree of anomaly2 for those feature values. A higher degree of anomaly indicates that the measured values2 deviate from the distribution observed under normal conditions.

Sen Linguistics

An anomaly detection device comprising: an acquisition unit that acquires a measured value1 from a sensor; a calculation unit that calculates a feature value1 from the measured value; an estimation unit that estimates a degree of anomaly2 from the feature value using a trained model; and an output unit that outputs an alarm when the degree of anomaly exceeds a threshold.

Paragraph [0015]
The estimation unit 14 inputs the feature value calculated by the calculation unit 13 into the trained model and estimates the degree of anomaly2 of the feature value. A higher degree of anomaly indicates that the measured value2 deviates further from the distribution in a normal state.

  1. 1Plurals. Japanese doesn’t mark them. The claim covers one measured value and one feature value. Google’s plurals could be read as requiring several, which changes what the claim covers.
  2. 2Consistency. Google calls 異常度 “an anomaly score” in the claim and “the degree of anomaly” in paragraph [0015]. 計測値 becomes “measurement values”, then “measured values”. The other side can argue that each pair means two different things.

3

Notes for counsel

Terms the other side could dispute, flagged first

The other side will check every word of the English. Notes cover language only. Claim construction stays with you.

Notes for counsel · JP 2019-000001 A

1. 異常度 (claim 1, paragraphs [0015]–[0021]): rendered “degree of anomaly” throughout. “Anomaly score” is common in the field, but a score suggests a number on a fixed scale. The reference doesn’t say the value has one.

2. 特徴量 (claim 1, paragraph [0015]): rendered “a feature value”, singular. The Japanese doesn’t mark number. Paragraph [0016] calculates one value from each measurement.
