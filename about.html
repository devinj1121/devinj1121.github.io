About

A translation company led by a scientist.

Founded in 2024, Sen Linguistics makes Chinese and Japanese documents hold up in US cases. For AI and language-tech roles, it also writes expert letters.

Founder

Devin Johnson, PhD

I’m a linguist and computer scientist. From 2021 to 2025, as a Data Science Fellow at Northwestern University, I researched how language models represent meaning and how people process sentences, and taught there.

Sen Linguistics also builds software: its own tools, and tools for client firms on request.

Working languages: English (native), Japanese (JLPT N1) and Chinese (advanced).

How a job runs

Every case gets a fixed quote and one set of terms.

See full samples of finished work.

  1. You send the documents. Every page, stamps included.
  2. You get a fixed quote. Nothing is charged before you approve it.
  3. You get the signed translation. Certified for USCIS, or with a signed declaration for court, reexam or the PTAB. Names and terms match across the case.
