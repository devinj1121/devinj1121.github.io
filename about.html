Founder

Devin Johnson, PhD

Devin is a linguist, computer scientist, and software developer. Since undergraduate studies at UW–Madison, he has built computer models that attempt to mechanistically explain how humans understand language and tested them with language experiments in English, Japanese, and Russian. From 2021 to 2025, he was a Data Science Fellow at Northwestern University and taught a variety of courses relating to linguistics, computation, and technical English. He now lives in Niigata, Japan.

  • PhD in Linguistics, Northwestern University2025
  • MS in Computational Linguistics, University of Washington2021
