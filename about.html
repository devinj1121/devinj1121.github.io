How we work

Every case gets a fixed quote and one set of terms.

The price is set before any work starts. Terms are checked against the field’s published English. Names and terms stay the same across every document in a case.

Patent work is done by a trained patent translator. Expert letters are written only for roles in the writer’s own field.

  • Evidence translation for EB-1, NIW and O-1 cases
  • Certified civil documents for every petition
  • Expert letters for AI and language-tech roles
  • Prior art for patent disputes, with a signed declaration
  • Drafting and checking tools for teams

For immigration lawyers

Who writes the expert letters

USCIS weighs an expert letter by who wrote it. It can discount a letter from an expert outside the applicant’s field, or one that isn’t tied to the record.

So Sen Linguistics writes letters only for roles in its founder’s field: natural language processing, language models, machine translation, conversational AI and applied AI. Devin Johnson writes and signs each one. The publications and research on this page are the record behind each letter.
