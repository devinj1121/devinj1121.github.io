In use every month

Built for our own patent work first

Sen Linguistics translates patentability reports for WIPO in monthly batches. Our own software fetches each report’s reference documents, drafts every segment, checks the draft twice, once for accuracy and once for English, and lists what a person needs to decide. The translator reviews every segment before delivery.
